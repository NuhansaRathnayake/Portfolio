*Sample for a fictional organisation, Northlight Studio. Not legal advice.*

## Purpose

This policy explains how staff use Northlight Studio's devices, accounts and data so that client work stays confidential and the studio stays running.

## Who it applies to

All employees, contractors and interns who use studio devices, email, cloud storage or client files.

## What staff must do

- Use only studio-approved accounts and storage for client files.
- Protect every account with a unique password and multi-factor authentication.
- Lock the screen when leaving a device and report a lost device the same day.
- Share client files only with people who need them for the project.

## What staff must not do

- Store client files in personal cloud accounts or on personal USB drives.
- Install software from untrusted sources on studio devices.
- Share logins with colleagues. Ask the office manager for access instead.

## Reporting and consequences

Report anything suspicious, such as an odd email or a missing file, to the office manager straight away. Reporting a mistake quickly is always better than hiding it. Repeated or deliberate breaches may lead to disciplinary action.

## Review

The director reviews this policy once a year and after any security incident.
