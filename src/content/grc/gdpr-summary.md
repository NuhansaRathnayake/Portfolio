## What it is

The General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) has applied since 25 May 2018. It sets rules for how organisations collect, use and protect personal data about people in the EU.

## Who it applies to

Organisations based in the EU, and organisations elsewhere that offer goods or services to people in the EU or monitor their behaviour. That is why a Sri Lankan software company with European customers may need to comply.

## Core principles

- Lawfulness, fairness and transparency
- Purpose limitation
- Data minimisation
- Accuracy
- Storage limitation
- Integrity and confidentiality
- Accountability

## What it means for a project

- Decide the lawful basis for processing before collecting data.
- Run a data protection impact assessment for high-risk processing.
- Build in ways to meet people's rights, such as access, correction and erasure.
- Notify the supervisory authority of a qualifying breach within 72 hours of becoming aware, where feasible.

## Penalties

The most serious breaches can lead to fines of up to €20 million or 4% of worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher.
