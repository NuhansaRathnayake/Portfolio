## What it is

The Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022 is Sri Lanka's first comprehensive data protection law. It creates a Data Protection Authority and sets out obligations for organisations that process personal data, and rights for the people the data is about.

## What it asks of organisations

The obligations will look familiar to anyone who has read the GDPR: processing must be lawful, for a specified purpose, limited to what is needed, accurate, kept no longer than necessary, kept secure, and handled transparently and accountably.

## Rights of individuals

People can ask to access their data, have it corrected or erased in certain cases, and withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing.

## Timing

Parts of the Act are being brought into force in stages. Anyone relying on this summary should check the Data Protection Authority's published timeline for the current position.

## What it means for a project

- Record what personal data a system collects, why, and who can see it.
- Collect less. Data you never gather cannot leak.
- Plan for requests to see, correct or delete data from the start, because adding it later is expensive.
