## What it is

The Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS) is a security standard from the PCI Security Standards Council. It applies to any organisation that stores, processes or transmits payment card data. Card brands and banks enforce it through their contracts rather than through a law.

## Version

At the time of writing, the current version is 4.0.1. Version 4.0 introduced new requirements that became mandatory on 31 March 2025. Check the PCI Security Standards Council website for the latest version.

## Twelve requirements, six goals

The standard groups its 12 requirements under six goals: build and maintain a secure network, protect account data, maintain a vulnerability management programme, implement strong access control, monitor and test networks, and maintain an information security policy.

## What it means for a project

- **Reduce scope.** Using a certified payment provider so card numbers never touch your own systems removes most of the burden.
- **Plan for evidence.** Compliance is proved with documents and logs, so build the evidence collection into the project plan.
- **Name an owner.** Someone must be accountable for the annual assessment.

*Summary for learning purposes. Merchants should confirm their requirements with their acquiring bank.*
