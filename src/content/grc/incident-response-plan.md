*Sample for a fictional organisation, Northlight Studio.*

## What counts as an incident

Anything that may expose client data or stop the studio working: a phishing email that was clicked, a lost laptop, a suspicious sign-in, ransomware or an unexpected file deletion.

## The six steps

1. **Report.** Whoever notices tells the office manager immediately, by phone if it is urgent.
2. **Assess.** The office manager decides how serious it is: is client data affected, and is work blocked?
3. **Contain.** Disconnect the affected device from the network, reset the affected passwords and revoke active sessions.
4. **Recover.** Restore files from the latest backup and confirm that everything works before reconnecting.
5. **Notify.** The director decides who to tell, including clients and any authority. Where personal data is involved, this decision is made within 72 hours of becoming aware.
6. **Learn.** Within one week, the team writes down what happened, why and what will change.

## Roles

| Role | Person | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Incident lead | Office manager | Runs steps 1 to 4 |
| Decision maker | Director | Approves notifications (step 5) |
| Technical support | IT contractor | Helps contain and recover |

## Keep updated

Review the contact details every six months and test the plan with a short walkthrough each year.
