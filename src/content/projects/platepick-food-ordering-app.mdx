## Overview

PlatePick is a food ordering app for Android. A customer opens the app, searches or browses the menu by category, adds meals to a cart and checks out. The idea was to keep the whole journey short: pick a plate, place the order.

## What it does

- Menu browsing with categories such as burgers, pizza, drinks and rice.
- Search to find a meal quickly.
- A cart that collects the chosen meals before checkout.

## My contribution

I worked on the app as part of a team and helped develop its ordering features. I also used GitHub to manage and update the project tasks, so the team could see what was done and what was still open.

## Tech stack

Java and Android Studio, with GitHub for version control and task tracking.

## What I learned

- Splitting an app into small tasks makes teamwork easier, because each person knows what they own.
- Keeping task status on GitHub gave the team one place to check progress instead of asking each other.
- Working through problems together improved both my problem-solving and my communication.
