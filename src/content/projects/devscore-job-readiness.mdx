## Overview

DevScore's idea is "Hire on proof, not promises." A resume lists skills, but a list is not evidence. DevScore matches the skills a candidate claims to what they have actually done on GitHub, and turns the result into a job-readiness score.

## What it does

- Gives each candidate a job-readiness score.
- Scores individual skills and shows how many of the claimed skills are verified.
- Marks skills as GitHub-verified when activity backs them up.
- Shows an evidence match, which is how closely the resume agrees with the real activity.

The figures on the project image are illustrative, to show how the dashboard looks.

## Why it matters

Hiring teams read many resumes and can rarely check every claim. Scoring against real activity gives them a faster and fairer starting point, and gives candidates a way to show what they can do.
